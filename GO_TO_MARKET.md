# TopDown positioning

## Category

TopDown is the account-intelligence workspace for complex B2B sales.

It turns scattered public research into a living account plan: who matters,
what is changing, how the deal connects, and what the team should do next.

## Outcome narrative

1. **Understand the account faster.** Start with a company domain and reach an
   evidence-backed, editable map without building the account plan by hand.
2. **Find a credible path into the deal.** Connect stakeholders, initiatives,
   and relationships while keeping sourced facts separate from hypotheses.
3. **Keep the team aligned on the next move.** Share one current view of account
   movement, research gaps, strategy, and prioritized actions.

## Audience

- **Sellers:** less account prep and more useful customer conversations.
- **Managers:** visible relationship coverage, research gaps, and next actions.
- **Revenue leaders:** a repeatable account-planning motion with aggregate,
  privacy-conscious evidence of adoption and action.

## Core proof story

Use a seller-first scenario: “I work at Stripe and I am selling into Microsoft.”

Show the transition from scattered tabs and a stale slide to:

- a researched, editable buying-group map;
- public evidence with confidence and freshness;
- an explicitly labeled relationship-path hypothesis;
- a current initiative connected to relevant roles;
- one prioritized, evidence-grounded next move;
- live measurement of time to a useful map and action taken.

The scenario is illustrative. Do not present illustrative roles, relationships,
or recommendations as verified facts about Microsoft.

## LinkedIn outreach

### Direct

I’m building TopDown, a faster way for sellers working complex accounts to
understand the buying group, see what changed, and decide the next move.

Could I build one of your live accounts with you and get 15 minutes of blunt
feedback? No pitch deck. We would use the product on a real account and see
where it is useful or wrong.

### Research-led

How much time does your team spend turning public research into account maps
that are stale a week later?

I’m building TopDown to turn a company domain into an evidence-backed account
plan, then keep the team aligned on who matters and what to do next. I’d be
glad to build one of your accounts with you in exchange for 15 minutes of
honest feedback.

### Manager

I’m working on a shared account-intelligence workspace for teams pursuing
complex deals. Reps get a researched stakeholder map and next actions; managers
get a current view of coverage, uncertainty, and account movement.

Would you be open to testing it on one live account for 15 minutes? I’m looking
for blunt feedback, not a polite demo audience.

## Claims discipline

Prefer observable product outcomes:

- time to an evidence-backed useful map;
- public sources reviewed;
- research refinements made;
- briefings opened and recommended actions selected;
- repeat account use;
- collaboration on live opportunity maps.

Do not promise a precise win-rate lift, guaranteed access to a buyer, verified
private relationships from public data, or a fixed amount of time saved.
